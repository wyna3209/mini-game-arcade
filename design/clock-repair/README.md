## 사용한 최종 프롬프트

### 후보 1

Create a polished landscape gameplay screenshot concept for a cozy Pokémon clock repair game. Hoothoot and Klink are the shop owners, with Pikachu visiting as a customer. Warm, charming pixel art wooden workshop interior. A very large front-facing readable analog clock with hour, minute and second hands is the central playable object. Include a compact top HUD with order 1/5, three hearts and coins, a clear digital target time 03:15:30, a countdown 28s, and three hand selector buttons plus a repair complete button beneath the clock. Small wooden shop sign in Korean: '부우부와 기어르의 시계 수리점'. This should look like a finished indie game with excellent art direction, brass and walnut details, friendly characters and clean functional UI. Single full game screen, no browser or device border. Candidate 1 of 3: warm pixel-art workshop.

Create a polished landscape gameplay screenshot concept for a cozy Pokémon clock repair game. Hoothoot and Klink jointly run a tiny forest clock workshop, and Eevee is the current visiting customer. Not pixel art. A large perfectly front-facing analog clock dominates the center with all twelve numerals in correct order and three clear colored hands with draggable ring tips. Keep it gameplay-readable and use no perspective on the clock face. Finished commercial indie game screenshot, not a website or poster. Coherent tasteful integrated game UI: compact top HUD order 2/5, three hearts, coins; readable digital target time 08:20:00 and a countdown 24s directly above the clock; three small hand selection tabs and a repair complete button below. Small hanging wood title sign in Korean '부우부와 기어르의 시계 수리점'. Korean button labels '시침', '분침', '초침', '수리 완료'. Keep decorations elegant and subordinate to the clock, excellent visual hierarchy, cozy magical forest mood. Full bleed single game canvas, no device or browser border, no watermarks.
